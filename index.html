01 · AI prototyping and entrepreneurship

Skillovna

A working EdTech MVP for SMEs that turns a skill assessment into structured, personalised results. I led the SME problem framing, made product decisions on what to include or cut, and co-built the automation that connects the full user journey.

Context
TUM AI Prototyping Course, team of 2
My contribution
Problem definition, product scope, user testing and technical build
Evidence
20+ interviews, 2 surveys and 3 prototype iterations

Why this was worth building

Small companies often have limited L&D capacity, while employees are left to find and evaluate training on their own. The team tested that problem before building and narrowed the product to one useful first step: identifying skill gaps and returning a structured starting point.

What changed through testing

  • The landing page went through three rounds of revision based on user feedback.
  • The feature set was reduced to keep the first user journey clear and testable.
  • Pitch feedback exposed an overstated market size and an unclear pricing structure, which informed the next iteration.

02 · Product design

Nexus

A two-sided platform for students searching for a thesis and supervisors managing applications. As Product Manager in a five-person team, I defined product requirements, structured the business model and helped translate research into the Figma prototype.

Context
TUM Product Design Course, cross-functional team of 5
My contribution
Product strategy, requirements, business model and stakeholder validation
Problem signal
8+ hours spent searching, with 90% of students dissatisfied

One platform, two different constraints

Students struggled to find suitable topics and track applications. Supervisors faced high demand, limited capacity and many applicants who did not meet the listed prerequisites. Treating only one side would have moved the bottleneck rather than removing it.

What the product had to support

  • Students need relevant topic discovery, clearer requirements and application status.
  • Supervisors need structured postings, candidate filtering and a manageable review queue.
  • The university needs visibility into demand and supervision capacity across chairs.

Two-sided platform concept

Nexus does not simply list topics. It coordinates matching, applications and progress across both sides of the thesis process.

03 · Master's thesis

AI Maturity Index

A transparent firm-level AI maturity measure built from corporate disclosures, patents, executive appointments, board composition and acquisition activity. The research covered 1,539 large firms from 2015 to 2024.

Context
TUM Chair of Strategy and Organisation, BCG × TUM research cluster
Core work
Data reconciliation, NLP features, predictive modelling and validation
Scale
1,539 firms across a ten-year observation window
Interactive companion

Explore the AI Maturity Index

Compare company scores, rankings, trends and feature contributions in the separate results application.

Open interactive app ↗

The methodological problem came first

Each source used a different company identifier. Before constructing features, I had to build a consistent firm panel and resolve naming variation, subsidiaries and unmatched records. That reconciliation step determined whether the later model could be trusted.

What the final analysis included

  • NLP features derived from corporate disclosure language.
  • Structured indicators from patents, leadership, boards and acquisitions.
  • Gradient-boosted trees for score construction and SHAP for feature attribution.
  • External validation against existing proprietary benchmarks.
